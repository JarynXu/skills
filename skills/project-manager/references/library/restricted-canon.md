# Restricted and externally licensed project-management canon

This file is a learning map, not a substitute for authorized editions. The works below are important to professional project management but are not bundled merely because they are influential.

## PMI body of knowledge and certification ecosystem

### PMBOK Guide / The Standard for Project Management

Learn from an authorized current edition when PMI terminology, process/standard alignment, organizational policy, exam preparation, or formal conformance matters.

Key learning goals:

- value delivery and system context;
- project-management principles and performance domains;
- tailoring;
- predictive, adaptive, and hybrid considerations;
- governance, stakeholders, team, planning, project work, delivery, measurement, uncertainty;
- models, methods, and artifacts as context-dependent tools rather than mandatory paperwork.

Do not reproduce PMI book text or certification-course content in this repository without explicit redistribution permission.

### Agile Practice Guide and PMI agile material

Use an authorized edition when the organization explicitly adopts PMI agile/hybrid terminology or when certification learning requires it. Pair it with the locally bundled Scrum/Kanban/GAO material so framework definitions remain grounded in their own canonical sources.

## PRINCE2

Use an authorized current manual when the project or customer formally uses PRINCE2 governance, management products, stages, tolerances, exception management, or certification terminology.

Learn the relationship between:

- business justification;
- roles and decision authority;
- stage boundaries and tolerances;
- product-based planning;
- risk, quality, issues, progress, and change;
- tailoring to project context.

Do not copy official manuals, exam material, or proprietary templates without permission.

## ISO project/programme/portfolio standards

Relevant standards include the ISO 21500 family such as ISO 21502 for project management. Consult licensed copies when a contract, audit, organization, or regulatory environment requires formal standards alignment.

Use the standard for terminology and conformance; use the authored references and open canon in this skill for executable working guidance. ISO text is not bundled because publication access does not grant open redistribution rights.

## Professional scheduling, EVM, and cost standards

Organizations may adopt proprietary or licensed standards for scheduling, earned value management systems, estimating, or work breakdown structures. The bundled GAO Schedule and Cost Guides provide substantial open/public guidance and point to many industry standards; consult the formally adopted standard when compliance is required.

## Commercial books and enduring professional classics

These books can deepen judgment but are copyrighted works. Learn concepts from authorized copies rather than copying chapters into the library.

- **The Mythical Man-Month** — communication, conceptual integrity, schedule fallacies, and adding people to late software work.
- **Peopleware** — team environment, focus, organizational dynamics, and knowledge-work productivity.
- **Critical Chain** — constraints, buffers, resource contention, and uncertainty; evaluate assumptions rather than treating it as universally applicable.
- **The Goal** — Theory of Constraints and system throughput thinking.
- **Accelerate** — empirical software-delivery performance research and organizational capabilities.
- **Team Topologies** — interaction modes, cognitive load, platform/stream-aligned team boundaries.
- **Thinking in Systems** — feedback, delays, stocks/flows, and unintended consequences useful for complex delivery systems.

These sources should influence professional judgment, not become a collection of unattributed quotes.

## Certification syllabi

PMP, PMI-ACP, PRINCE2, Scrum certifications, SAFe, ITIL, and other credentials may define proprietary exam objectives or courseware. Certification competence is not equivalent to project competence. Use official authorized learning material when certification alignment is requested; keep this skill oriented toward project decisions, evidence, and delivery outcomes.

## Contribution rule

Before vendoring any work currently listed here:

1. identify the exact edition/artifact;
2. confirm the license applies to that exact artifact, not merely an adjacent website or code repository;
3. preserve attribution, notices, and modification requirements;
4. record source/version/hash in the catalog;
5. preprocess it into agent-ready Markdown when format permits;
6. add tests proving it is discoverable and does not silently replace project-specific authority.
