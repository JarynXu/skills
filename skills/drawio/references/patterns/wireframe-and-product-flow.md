# Wireframes and product flows

Use native mockup or basic shapes for low-fidelity screens, navigation states, dialogs, forms, and user/system transitions. Draw screen boundaries clearly and label actions on transitions.

A product flow shows state/navigation logic; a wireframe shows interface structure. Keep them separate when combining both would make either unreadable. Do not pretend draw.io low-fidelity shapes are a final high-fidelity UI design system.
